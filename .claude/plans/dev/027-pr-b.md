**Title:** feat: plan 027 — M8 PR B: cart trigger panels, tracking warnings, the 5 Recover carts templates, canvas scroll

## Summary

The UI of M8 (ROADMAP-dev M8.6–M8.8 and Templates), on top of the backend merged in #32. Merchants can now pick the cart triggers, see what they do, get told when cart tracking is off, and start from 5 Recover carts templates.

Base: `develop` e3df6e8. Frontend and docs, plus one backend change: unsubscribing stops a cart run. No schema change. One commit per checkpoint.

## Checkpoints

  - Both cart trigger panels get notes: when a cart counts as abandoned (minutes from Settings), that a cart never starts both triggers, that runs stop on an order, who is skipped, and a warning with a link while tracking is off.
  - The picker draws trigger sections (Order, Carts) like actions. A step with no category has no heading.
  - `withServerIssues` lets `cart_tracking_off` through, for the activate issue and the draft issue an Update returns. The issue bar and the overview offer Open Settings. The panel leaves the warning to its notes, without a red notice.
  - Library badge "Needs cart tracking" and Before you start in the preview (`needsCartTracking`).
  - Run detail links the order that stopped a run ("View order").
  - `useSaveSettings` writes `wc.cartTracking` and `wc.abandonMinutes` into the page meta, so all of this follows Settings without a reload.

## Worth a closer look
- `useSaveSettings` removes cached workflows when tracking changes. `useAutosave` keeps the issues of the copy it first gets, so a stale `cart_tracking_off` would otherwise stay in the editor.
- `stoppedByOrderUrl` is its own line under the steps: the reason may sit on a step's row.

## Known limits
- With tracking off, an inactive workflow with a draft on the same trigger keeps Update disabled in error mode, though the server would accept it. Open Settings is one click away.
- Settings saved in another tab show after a reload, as for all Settings.
- Unsubscribing finds carts by their current email: a cart whose email changed after its series started (a different address typed at checkout) keeps running for the new address.
- Unsubscribing cancels the whole cart run, including any Send internal email or fixed-address email after the wait.
